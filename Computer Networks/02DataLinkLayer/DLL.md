# Data link Layer (DLL)
    - We divide this into 2 sub layers. 
        - Top is called "Logical Link Control (LLC)"
            - Flow control, error control and part of framing duties are collected in this. 
        - Bottom is called "Media Access Control"
            - It defines specific access method for each LAN, Ethernet and take care of Addressing at the level (LAN technology).
            - some duties of framing is handled here as well.


- Ye Local level par LAN/Ethernet Addressing krta hai jise MAC address kahte hai.
